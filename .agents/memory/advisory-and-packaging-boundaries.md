---
name: Advisory and packaging boundaries
description: Evidence and manufacturing-claim limits requested for AI and 3D packaging
---

An entry such as “180 kg setup/layout” can be an investigation lead, never an
established cause without supporting records and operator evidence. Keep recorded
facts, calculations, hypotheses and missing evidence visibly distinct.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritizes trustworthy assistance over feature count
and prohibits unsupported causal claims, fabricated measurements and silent AI
changes to production data.

**How to apply:** Apply this standard to every future summary, alert, recommendation
and AI action. Advisory output does not become an authoritative production record.

A 3D packaging concept is not a validated dieline or manufacturing instruction.
Do not infer cutting pieces from arbitrary 3D shapes or weight measurements.

**Why:** The user wants an extensible general packaging designer, not a visually
convincing demo that claims unsupported manufacturing correctness.

**How to apply:** Require explicit geometry/fold/manufacturing validation and
user-approved, permission-checked integration before transferring designs into
production cutting. Preserve existing rectangular Cutting Studio workflows.
