## 4. Finish and verify every core workflow

### Authentication and roles
Verify login, logout, session persistence, page refresh, CSRF protection, password replacement, session revocation, throttling, and appropriate error handling.

Test all three roles using real test accounts:

- Cutting Operator
- Production Supervisor
- Manager

Verify both frontend route restrictions and backend/API authorization. Hiding a button or page is not sufficient security. Operators must receive a proper denial for restricted reports, comparisons, employee administration, and unauthorized stock movements. Supervisors and Managers must have only the permissions intended for their roles. Confirm employee provisioning and management work without introducing insecure default credentials.

### Production jobs and material reconciliation
Verify job creation and lifecycle, material issues, production entries, scrap categories, reusable offcuts, rounding precision, variance, yield, and scrap-rate calculations. Test invalid quantities, missing data, duplicate submissions, corrections, closed jobs, and attempts to close jobs with unresolved material or rework.

### Rework
Verify that pending rework is visible and traceable; that it can be resolved into good output or reasoned scrap; that corrections retain audit history; and that a job cannot be closed while required rework remains unresolved. Prevent double counting when rework changes status or is corrected.

### Offcut inventory
Verify registration, reservation, release, consumption, receiving-job linkage, stock balances, movement history, revision protection, and concurrent update handling.

Balances must be mathematically consistent: available stock, reserved stock, consumed stock, and remaining stock must not be counted twice. A receiving-job issue must be traceable and must not silently alter the original job's material reconciliation. Preserve the existing protections against unsupported reversals or independent voiding of accounting movements; implement reversals only if a complete, audited, safe workflow is supported by the existing design and requirements.

### Cutting Studio
Verify layout creation, piece placement, dragging, touch interactions, keyboard controls, rotation, numeric position inputs, validation, saving, reopening, revisions, and accurate restoration of manually positioned pieces. Confirm invalid or out-of-bounds positions are clearly rejected rather than silently changed.

Do not claim industrial cutting optimization, machine-ready output, or manufacturing-grade nesting unless the implementation genuinely supports and validates it.

### Packaging
Verify packaging concepts can be created, saved, reopened, edited, versioned, and exported. Confirm dimensions drive the preview and that saved dimensions and exported data agree. Verify validation and error handling.

Treat packaging previews as conceptual unless manufacturing-grade dielines and their correctness are independently implemented and validated. Do not present conceptual packaging as production-ready CAD.

### Dashboards, reports, and navigation
Verify role-specific landing pages, filters, totals, report calculations, source-record links, pagination, empty states, loading states, and error states. Ensure dashboard summaries agree with the underlying records and cannot expose data that the current role is not allowed to see.

Remove or correct confusing, redundant, misleading, or unfinished UI text where it materially harms the workflow. Keep the interface clear and professional without starting a large visual redesign or adding decorative features unrelated to acceptance.

### Arabic and English
Verify language switching, Arabic right-to-left layout, English left-to-right layout, forms, tables, navigation, validation messages, dialogs, and mobile layouts. Fix broken, missing, or incorrectly directed text that is discovered during testing.

### Optional AI assistant
Keep AI optional and unobtrusive. If no valid provider configuration exists, show a clear unavailable state and do not display a fake assistant or fabricated response. If configured, verify that it uses authorized application records, explains the evidence behind observations, and does not claim an unproven root cause. Do not expose credentials or make core application workflows depend on AI availability.
