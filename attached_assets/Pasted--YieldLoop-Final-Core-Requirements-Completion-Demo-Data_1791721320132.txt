## 1. Source of truth: original challenge requirements

The core product is a cutting material yield tool for Ibn Khaldoun Printing and Publishing.

Its purpose is to show where board or paper goes in every cutting job and identify the largest avoidable material losses without counting reusable offcuts as waste.

The original challenge requires the application to support:

1. A digital production job card.
2. Material and cutting machine selection.
3. Recording issued material weight.
4. Recording good product output.
5. Recording scrap separately by reason.
6. Recording reusable offcuts separately from scrap.
7. Recording rework separately when applicable.
8. Rejecting unbalanced job records.
9. Showing yield and scrap with explicit definitions.
10. Comparing two jobs using the same machine.
11. Opening the underlying records to investigate differences.
12. A factory demonstration using the two supplied case records.
13. Optional AI assistance that suggests investigations without inventing a root cause.

All existing features must be assessed against these requirements. A feature is complete only when its actual behavior is implemented and verified, not merely documented or represented by a placeholder.

## 2. Required material reconciliation and calculations

Enforce this reconciliation rule using the existing authoritative backend business logic:

Issued material = good output + total scrap + reusable offcuts

Handle decimal quantities precisely and consistently. Do not use floating-point approximations for material accounting.

Definitions must be consistent throughout the backend, UI, reports, dashboard, and comparisons:

- **Issued material:** total material issued to the job.
- **Good output:** weight of acceptable product recorded for the job.
- **Scrap:** material classified as waste in this exercise.
- **Reusable offcuts:** material retained for potential reuse, not scrap.
- **Total scrap:** sum of the recorded scrap categories.
- **Scrap rate:** total scrap ÷ issued material × 100%.
- **Good yield:** good output ÷ issued material × 100%.

Reusable offcuts must not be counted as scrap.

Do not confuse scrap rate with the percentage of all material that did not become good output. Good yield and scrap rate are separate metrics, and the reusable fraction must remain visible.

### Validation behavior

- Reject or prevent closing a job as balanced when its quantities do not reconcile.
- Perform authoritative validation on the server, not only in the frontend.
- Provide clear explanations when a job is incomplete or unbalanced.
- Support the existing correction, void, and replacement workflows without double-counting records.
- Prevent unauthorized users from bypassing validation.
- Handle zero, negative, missing, or invalid quantities appropriately.
- Avoid silently rounding away material differences.
- Preserve a clear audit trail for material-related changes.

If rework is implemented as a separate record type, define and enforce its accounting treatment so it cannot be double-counted. The original case data supplies no rework quantities, so do not invent any.

## 7. AI assistance — evidence-based investigation only

Inspect whether a real AI Assistant is implemented and functional. If it is missing or incomplete, implement the minimum useful AI assistance required by the original brief.

The AI should help explain production results and identify possible investigation leads.

### Mandatory example

For IK-202, the assistant may say:

"The recorded data shows 180 kg of setup/layout scrap, the largest recorded scrap category for this job. This is a useful area to investigate. Review the cutting instructions, layout, setup records, material issue, and operator observations to determine whether the setup or layout contributed to the loss. The available figures alone do not establish the exact root cause."

It must not say that setup or layout definitely caused the loss without sufficient supporting evidence.

### Required behavior

- Base summaries on actual authorized records.
- Distinguish recorded facts, calculated results, possible explanations, and missing evidence.
- Cite relevant job references and recorded categories when available.
- Suggest concrete follow-up checks.
- Support Arabic and English.
- Never fabricate data, operator statements, measurements, or root causes.
- Never treat correlation as proof of causation.
- Never silently alter production records or inventory.
- Enforce role-based access and backend validation.
- Keep AI provider credentials server-side.
- Use a real provider when correctly configured.
- If credentials are absent, show an honest configuration/unavailable state instead of simulating real AI.

Do not build an elaborate autonomous agent or complex AI workflow beyond what is necessary for trustworthy production assistance.
