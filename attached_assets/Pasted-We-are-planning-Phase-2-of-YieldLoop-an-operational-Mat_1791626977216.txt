We are planning Phase 2 of YieldLoop, an operational Material Yield & Production Intelligence Platform for Ibn Khaldoun Printing and Publishing.

**This is a READ-ONLY planning and inspection task. Do not modify, create, delete, or overwrite any files. Do not run migrations, alter the database, install packages, or change configuration.**

## Confirmed scope

YieldLoop currently serves one printing business: Ibn Khaldoun Printing and Publishing. Do not introduce multi-tenant organization onboarding or unrelated roles.

The only application roles are:

1. Cutting Operator
2. Production Supervisor
3. Manager

The Manager (or designated authorized person under the approved account-management policy) creates employee accounts and assigns their roles. There is no public self-registration.

The current foundation uses React + TypeScript + Vite on the frontend, Django + Django REST Framework on the backend, and PostgreSQL. Preserve this stack and the existing architecture.
